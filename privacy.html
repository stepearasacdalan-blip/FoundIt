Privacy and safe returns

FoundIt shares enough public information to help people recognize an item while keeping ownership clues private.

What appears on a public report

Public reports may show the item title, category, public description, color, approximate date, general campus area, and report status. Keep descriptions broad enough that they don’t reveal proof of ownership.

Do not post: phone numbers, student IDs, serial numbers, exact storage locations, passwords, account recovery answers, or other private identifiers.

Private claim details

When you claim an item, add private details that only its owner would know. These details are not part of the public listing. The claimant, the report owner reviewing the claim, and an authorized staff reviewer may see claim information in the Recovery Center.

Google Sheets stores the project’s records. The spreadsheet owner can access those records, so keep the sheet private and only use information you need for verification.

Possible matches are not proof

SmartMatch compares category, shared words, color, approximate date, and general campus area. A match is a suggestion for people to review. It does not establish who owns an item.

Meet safely and confirm together

Arrange handovers in a staffed, public campus location. Do not meet in isolated places or share unnecessary personal information. After a claim is approved, the claimant and authorized finder each confirm the handover in FoundIt.

Prototype limits

This is an academic case-study project, not an official campus service or production-ready system. It does not send email or push notifications. Do not use it for urgent, highly sensitive, or high-value property without campus approval and a security review.

If you are testing, remove test records from the private spreadsheet when finished. For privacy questions, contact the person managing this FoundIt project.
